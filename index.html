How to Play

  • Move: Arrow keys or WASD
  • Interact: Walk up to an NPC or object — a prompt appears automatically
  • Hint (Tier 3+): Hold H to peek at subtitles (limited charges/day)
  • Tap words: In Tier 2, tap a highlighted word in a subtitle for its meaning
  • Quest Log: Q
  • Save: autosaves as you play, per language
